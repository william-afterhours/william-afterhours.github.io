# Barrow Neurological Institute, Phoenix — the place of the 1991 operation

## 1. One line

Neurosurgical institute within St Joseph's Hospital and Medical Center in Phoenix, Arizona, where Robert Spetzler's team performed operations under hypothermic circulatory arrest from the 1980s. Pam Reynolds was operated on there in August 1991. The only photograph with a usable licence shows a tower built later and named after the surgeon, so it is used as a reference picture only.
