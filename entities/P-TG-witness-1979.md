# T.G. — CCU nurse at the Canisius Hospital, sole witness of the "dentures" case

## 1. One line

The male nurse who led the resuscitation of B. in 1979. He is the person who took the dentures out and put them on the cart's tray, and the person who, more than a week later, heard B.'s words. Public records too use his initials only, at his request (Smit 2008 p. 50). Status unknown → initials.
