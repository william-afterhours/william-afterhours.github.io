# Florence Barrett (Lady Barrett) — obstetric surgeon, first recorder of the 1924 case

## 1. One line

British obstetric surgeon (1867–1945). In January 1924 she attended Doris's delivery at the Mothers' Hospital, Clapton, listened at the bedside while the patient described her vision, and wrote and signed a note "immediately afterwards". She was William Barrett's wife: the strength of a physician's observational record and the independence weakness of being the investigator's wife meet in one person. A public figure whose death is confirmed, so named.
