# Keith Augustine — sceptical author of the 2007 JNDS survey of veridical-perception claims

## 1. One line

American writer on philosophy of mind and religion who published in 2007, in three parts in the *Journal of Near-Death Studies*, a critical survey of the evidence for paranormal perception in NDEs from a naturalist position. The first part treats the Pam Reynolds case at length and introduced the point that card 3 rests on: the checked perceptions occurred under ordinary anaesthesia, hours away from the period of a silent brain. A public author, so named. His texts have no witnesses of their own, so as counter-evidence they are capped at C.
