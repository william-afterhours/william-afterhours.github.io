# Ji Yun — the Qing scholar who wrote down Lady Zhang's last words

## 1. One line

Qing official and scholar (1724-07-26 – 1805-03-14, Wikidata Q711989). In the preface of *Guwang tingzhi* (1793) he says that "after fifty I led the editing of the palace books", which is his part in the imperial library and its catalogue (the Siku project, 1772–1782). Author of the *Yuewei caotang biji*, five collections of strange accounts (1789–1798, printed together 1800), about 1,200 entries, most collected from friends and colleagues and others from his own experience (Wikipedia), many with the informant named. This study tracks him as a **recorder**: he heard accounts in person and published them under his name, with a declared moral purpose ("not to run against moral teaching") and without checking them.
