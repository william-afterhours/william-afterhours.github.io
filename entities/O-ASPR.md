# American Society for Psychical Research — investigating-body code ASPR

## 1. One line

The American counterpart of the SPR, founded in Boston in 1885 and re-founded in New York in 1906 under James H. Hyslop (dates received, not verified from a primary source). Its *Journal* (from 1907) printed first-hand records and letters, often with the editor's notes on provenance. For this study it is a separate investigating body from the London SPR (METHOD §5: "different institutions are different bodies"), which matters for the two-bodies condition of the convergence verdict. A case printed by the ASPR after the fact (such as the 1883 Browning letter) is not "collected" by it; the body affects S6 only when it gathered or interviewed.
