# James H. Hyslop — the ASPR's editor and collector

## 1. One line

American professor of logic and ethics at Columbia (1854–1920), who ran the American Society for Psychical Research from its re-foundation in 1906 until his death as secretary, treasurer, editor and chief investigator. The *Journal of the ASPR* under him printed deathbed-vision records, among them the 1883 Browning letter (1909) and a group of cases in 1918 that Barrett later reprinted. As an investigator who published in support of survival, his collections score at most 1 on S6, and cases printed by him share one investigating body (route ⑤) with one another, not with records that predate his handling of them. Public author, death confirmed, so named.
