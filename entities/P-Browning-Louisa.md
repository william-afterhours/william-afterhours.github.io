# Louisa Browning — the dying woman of the 1883 letter

## 1. One line

An Englishwoman who died in Kensington, London, early on 28 October 1883, aged 78, two days after a fall, after many years of diabetes. Sister of Amelia Jane Ericsson (d. 1867), wife of the engineer John Ericsson, and aunt of Ellen, Lady Chute, who wrote the letter that records her last words. The experiencer of [[DBV-GB-1883-browning]]. Death confirmed in the civil index, so named. Not to be confused with the schoolmistress Louisa Browning (d. 1887) of the Wikipedia article.

## 5. Notes

- Maiden name unknown. Amelia's Wikidata entry (Q104852892) gives the surname Byam without dates; if Louisa was Amelia's full sister, "Browning" is a married name and Ellen Browning of Auckland was related through Louisa's husband. Not pursued; no effect on the grade.
- Her address in Kensington, her household and the names of "those watching by her" are unknown. A probate calendar entry (1883–84) would give the address; not searched.
