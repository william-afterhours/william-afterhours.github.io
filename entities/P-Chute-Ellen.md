# Ellen Chute — the niece who wrote the 1883 letter

## 1. One line

Born a Browning, "of Auckland" (New Zealand); married General Sir Trevor Chute (1816–1886), K.C.B., on 9 July 1868; living at "Egmont", Bracknell, Berkshire, in 1883. Author of the letter of 5 November 1883 to John Ericsson that records her aunt Louisa Browning's last words. She was **not present** at the death (Kensington) and reports what "those watching by her" heard. Married in 1868, so long dead; her exact death date is unverified. Named as a historical person.
