# Michael B. Sabom — cardiologist, investigator of the Pam Reynolds case

## 1. One line

American cardiologist (Atlanta). One of the first physicians to compare patients' accounts of their own resuscitation with the medical record (*Recollections of Death*, 1982, unverified). His second book, *Light & Death* (1998, unverified, quoted at length in the JNDS papers), presents the Pam Reynolds case, which he investigated alone: he interviewed her from 1994 and obtained the operative record and the surgeons' explanations. A public author, so named. He is sympathetic to the survival hypothesis, and in 2007 wrote that his certainty about life after death rests on his Christian faith, not on NDE research, and that NDE accounts are "not accounts of life after death". Sole investigator of card 3 → S6 = 1.

## 5. Notes

- His own disclaimers, as quoted in the 2007 exchange: the Reynolds case gives "tantalizing clues but no definite answers" (1998 pp. 189–190); medical science "cannot raise people from the dead" (pp. 49–50); NDEs are "not accounts of life after death" (p. 198). An investigator who writes this is not inflating his record; the inflation came later, in the popular retellings.
