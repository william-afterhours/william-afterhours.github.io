# William Fletcher Barrett — physicist, founder of the SPR, collector of deathbed visions

## 1. One line

British physicist (1844–1925). He led the founding of the Society for Psychical Research (SPR) in 1882 and collected deathbed visions and crisis apparitions, printing them in *On the Threshold of the Unseen* (1917) and the posthumous *Death-bed Visions* (1926). As an investigator who openly supported the survival hypothesis, the cases he collected score at most 1 on S6, and cases in the same book share one investigating body (route ⑤). A public author whose death is confirmed, so named.
