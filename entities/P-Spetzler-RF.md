# Robert F. Spetzler — the neurosurgeon who operated on Pam Reynolds

## 1. One line

American neurosurgeon at the Barrow Neurological Institute, Phoenix. His 1988 paper describes the technique used in 1991: deep hypothermia, circulatory arrest, drainage of the blood and barbiturate protection, with brainstem auditory evoked potentials (clicks played into both ears) and cortical potentials monitored until they flatten. He performed the 1991 operation and, in the 2002 BBC documentary, said he had no explanation for her perceptions. A public author, so named; living as far as known, so no portrait is used (and he is a witness in the case).
