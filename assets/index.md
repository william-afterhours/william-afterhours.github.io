# List of materials

Every photograph, document scan, newspaper, illustration and diagram attached to a case card or entity, with its source and licence. The rules are in [[METHOD]] §9, "rules for visual materials". The essentials again:

- **Pictures help understanding; they are not evidence.** They have no effect on the grades (R, V, I). The exception is a scan of a primary document (a copy of a record), which is the proof that a source was "opened".
- **Because the repository is public, only public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA and government works.** Copyrighted papers, books and photographs are linked, not included. Copyrighted pictures are not "redrawn" either: copying the expression still makes a derivative work, and above all such a picture is not a record but an invention. Instead the **facts** (sequence, layout, numbers, map) are redrawn as diagrams. That is lawful and useful to this study.
- **Every item is opened and looked at before use.** The caption says only what the picture actually shows. Anything that is not the site or the person of the event is marked "reference".
- **Newspapers** are the best copies of records (recorded right after the event). Archive, newspaper, date, page and article URL are all recorded. If a search found nothing, that too goes into §13 of the card.
- Never used: generative images, re-enactment photographs, images of unknown provenance, faces of witnesses or experiencers who are alive or whose status is unknown.
- File name `<card or entity id>--<slug>.<ext>`, long side ≤ 1600 px, ≤ 400 KB.

Tools: `tools/commons_fetch.py` (checks the licence on Wikimedia Commons, fetches metadata and a reduced copy), `tools/diagrams/<id>.py` (diagram code, SVG output).
